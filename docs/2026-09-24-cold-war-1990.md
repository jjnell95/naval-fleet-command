# Cold War 1990

24 September 2026. Built from main commit `005d21c` in an isolated local checkout.

The game now opens around the commander's task: choose an operation, read the first orders, form a contact picture and protect the mission's objective. Four fictional September 1990 operations use a separate period catalogue and original class recognition art. The modern missions remain available.

## Play this version

Double-click **Launch Preview.command** in the source folder to serve the included browser build locally. It opens a browser and prints its local address; close the terminal to stop. Alternatively, open `project.godot` in Godot 4.7.2. Start with **Cold War 1990 → Northern Convoy → Read briefing → Take command**.

The merchant already heads toward the handover at 14 knots. Select Elrod, inspect the track list and keep its air-search radar active. Use Nicholas to screen the approach. Space pauses, G plots a route, B expands the chart, F3 opens Air Operations, and Command-K or Control-K finds commands. Higher time compression is useful between contacts.

## What changed

The mission desk shows period, difficulty, command role, intent, first orders, chart and a period ship portrait. Orders and objectives have their own briefing page, with situation and controls on separate tabs. Page and mission changes start at the top, while live objective updates preserve the reading position. Mission and contact rows now have readable primary and secondary lines with native keyboard selection.

The command strip is actionable: open mission status, cycle the current contact filter, focus a detected inbound, open air operations, or expand the chart. The roster has more space, the portrait consumes less, and the command dock fits its controls. Contact counts and cycling respect the selected ship's actual information and the active filter.

The additions include 20 period platforms, 28 weapons, 25 sensors, 136 recognition images and 48 inspectable models. Perry, Spruance, Ticonderoga, Nimitz, Los Angeles, Victor III, Sovremennyy, Udaloy, Slava and Nanuchka appear alongside period aviation. The full catalogue now has 94 platforms, 98 weapons, 106 sensors and 192 models across 15 missions.

The scenario tests exposed AI defects that previously obscured the tactical problem. Torpedo-only submarines now count ammunition they can actually use. ASW patrols begin spaced buoy fields after reaching their first station; aircraft search lost-contact areas, and dipping helicopters resume their next leg. SH-60B no longer attempts to dip without a dipping set. A breakout surface ship follows its route while retaining or establishing its own radar picture.

## Historical standard

The [source and model ledger](COLD_WAR_1990.md) separates period evidence from estimates. The carrier uses the F-14A+ name appropriate to Eisenhower's 1990 deployment, and the escort fits distinguish Mk 13, dated Mk 41 refits, hull sonar and passive towed arrays. The new missions do not silently inherit modern aircraft or weapons.

These are alternate-history deployments and battles. Performance values, acoustic signatures, ammunition allocations, aircraft detachments and timing are estimates. Models emphasize class silhouettes rather than exact small fittings. Shared tactical data is more immediate than period communications; continuous illumination, common-rail conflicts and some weapon trajectories remain simplified. Full logistics, replenishment and save games are outside this release. This is a stronger command game, not a validated reconstruction of real naval combat.

## Verification

All **308 regression tests** pass. The real-scene interface suite includes briefing transitions, filtered contacts, chart layout, pause state and air-operations access; existing gallery and modal checks also pass. The machine-readable [M21 validation record](validation-m21.json) records final sizes and browser checks.

The four new missions were run through resolution with both sides under AI at seeds 2 and 13, plus passive-player convoy and ASW runs. All ten resolved with no script/runtime errors or sampled grounded actors. Convoy AI and passive protection won; Dallas fired torpedoes and patrols laid buoys in the ASW wins, while the passive ASW screen lost. Carrier outcomes varied by seed. Both Baltic AI runs lost, consistent with its Advanced rating, and do not establish human difficulty or optimal tactics. See the [complete scenario evidence](validation-cold-war-scenarios.json).

All eleven existing missions additionally passed 1,200 simulated seconds at seed 2 without errors, warnings or grounding. These are stability checks, not full mission completions. The displayed 20–30 minute play times are design estimates using time compression, not measured human completion times.

This deliverable is local. Publishing to GitHub and replacing the public browser build require a separate approved step and fresh deployment verification.
