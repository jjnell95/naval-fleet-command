# Crew radio and sound effects

14 November 2026 · Godot 4.7.2

Crew reports previously depended on the device's text-to-speech engine, with additional pitch and
speed changes to distinguish ships. Effects were mostly single tones or short noise bursts; guns,
torpedoes and missiles shared one launch cue. The machinery loops repeated every second, and the
background did not make room for spoken reports.

The replacement uses 76 complete, offline-rendered radio performances in two stable voices, with
light communications filtering and no playback pitch shifts. Short acknowledgements, contact
reports and warnings retain the late-1990s command-room style. Exact ship names, track numbers and
refusal reasons remain in the visual radio log. Incoming-threat reports do not imply automatic
engagement, and returning aircraft no longer always claim a fuel emergency.

Missile ignition, gunfire, torpedo tubes and bomb releases now have different effects. Layered
transients and rumble replace the basic combat noises, with three takes for common combat effects.
Console feedback is softer, sonar and threat cues remain recognisable, and mission results use
crew reports with a short confirmation cue instead of musical jingles. Five ambient loops run
for 10–18 seconds. Background sound and routine effects drop during speech; threat warnings retain
their own playback slot and stay audible during a busy salvo.

Playback works offline on Linux, Windows, macOS and the browser. Existing sound preferences,
Ctrl-M, alert priority and time-compression rules remain. Muting discards waiting reports. A
mission result waits for the current transmission to finish. Tests and scripted walkthroughs stay
silent unless they explicitly install the audio-check playback sink.

The bank is about 1.6 MB. These are generated voices and authored effects, not recordings from
Jane's Fleet Command or hired actors. [Asset provenance and rebuild instructions](../assets/audio/README.md)
describe the stock Kokoro voices and the sound-design process. No model or network service is
required to play the game.
