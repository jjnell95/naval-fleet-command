Build the picture

Radar horizons, sonar bearings and electronic support. Contacts classify over time, carry their plot history, and become uncertain when lost.

Manage the shield

Layered missile defence, finite magazines, decoys, fire-control channels and a live threat-evaluation board with time to impact.

Inspect every platform

Orbit 56 ship, submarine and aircraft models. Explore their sensor fits and loadouts, then inspect 58 weapon systems. Every model is original illustrative game art.

Ballistic missile defence

Aero-ballistic Kinzhal shots come in high and fast. SM-3 is reserved for exoatmospheric targets; the existing lower-altitude Kinzhal profile requires a suitable terminal interceptor. Keep the cruiser inside the screen with its BMD magazine intact.

Electronic attack

The EA-18G Growler degrades hostile radars looking through it. Jammed bearings show on the picture, and the jammer is the loudest thing on the air for the other side's ESM.

Sea state

A rough sea shortens passive sonar and hides sea-skimmers in clutter. Arctic Shield is fought in sea state 4.

Scenario editor

Build your own mission in the game: place any of the 56 platforms on the chart, set routes, objectives and sea state, save it in your browser, share it as JSON, and play it straight away.

Damage control

Crews restore knocked-out propulsion, sensors and launchers over time, to a cap. The hull stays damaged.

Coastlines

Ten missions use Natural Earth geographic coastlines, separate islands and straits, and latitude/longitude grids. Ships cannot enter it, high ground masks radar, ESM and sonar above the sight line, and a sea-skimmer that meets a headland ends there. Aircraft fly over. Trace your own coastline in the editor.

Your first watch

Choose Aegis Bastion, read the briefing, then Take Command. Select USS Dwight D. Eisenhower, open Aviation + ASW and press Launch for early warning. Click your destroyer to inspect its systems and explore its 3D model; right-click water to set a course. When you are ready for the regimental raid, play Arctic Shield.

An independent fleet-command game, unaffiliated with Jane's or any armed service. Gameplay estimates, simplified carrier operations, and a local NM grid. Jamming, ballistic flight, terrain masking and sea state are abstractions, and terrain heights are estimated. Coastlines derive from public-domain Natural Earth 1:10m data, generalized for this game. Bathymetry, replenishment and save games remain future work. See the repository for model limitations and sources.
