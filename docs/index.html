Build the picture

Radar horizons, sonar bearings and electronic support. Contacts classify over time, carry their plot history, and become uncertain when lost.

Manage the shield

Layered missile defence, finite magazines, decoys, fire-control channels and a live threat-evaluation board with time to impact.

Command across domains

Launch the E-2D, station the Growler on the raid axis, direct fighter sorties, deploy sonobuoys and screen the carrier with ships and a submarine.

Ballistic missile defence

Aero-ballistic Kinzhal shots come in high and fast. Only the SM-3 layer meets them outside the atmosphere; SM-6 is a terminal fallback. Keep the cruiser inside the screen with its BMD magazine intact.

Electronic attack

The EA-18G Growler degrades hostile radars looking through it. Jammed bearings show on the picture, and the jammer is the loudest thing on the air for the other side's ESM.

Sea state

A rough sea shortens passive sonar and hides sea-skimmers in clutter. Arctic Shield is fought in sea state 4.

Scenario editor

Build your own mission in the game: place any of the 30 platforms on the chart, set routes, objectives and sea state, save it in your browser, share it as JSON, and play it straight away.

Damage control

Crews restore knocked-out propulsion, sensors and launchers over time, to a cap. The hull stays damaged.

Coastlines

Seven missions are charted with land. Ships cannot enter it, high ground masks radar, ESM and sonar above the sight line, and a sea-skimmer that meets a headland ends there. Aircraft fly over. Trace your own coastline in the editor.

Your first watch

Choose Aegis Bastion, read the briefing, then Take Command. Select Carrier Resolute and press Launch for early warning. Click your destroyer to inspect its systems; right-click water to set a course. When you are ready for the regimental raid, play Arctic Shield.

An independent fleet-command game, unaffiliated with Jane's or any armed service. Gameplay estimates, simplified carrier operations, and a local NM grid. Jamming, ballistic flight, terrain masking and sea state are abstractions, and every coastline is invented. Bathymetry, replenishment and save games remain future work.
