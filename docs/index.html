A chart with a floor

Natural Earth bathymetry under every mission: shelf and basin tint, relief, and anti-aliased 200–4000 m contours. The coast carries on past the charted area instead of ending in a clip line.

Water that hides boats

A thermal layer that hull sonars cannot hear through, variable-depth and dipping sets that go under it, muffled shelf water, convergence zones in the deep basins, and a sea floor no submarine can dive past.

A fight for the ship

Hits start fires and flooding that damage control wins or loses over the next hour. A consort alongside can save a burning ship. Chaff moves a missile, and it may find the ship behind.

Your first watch

Choose Aegis Bastion, read the briefing, then Take Command. Select a ship, press G, and left-click water to plot a move. Hook a contact to open its engagement solution. If the picture gets away from you, click the Tactical Overview or press Home.
